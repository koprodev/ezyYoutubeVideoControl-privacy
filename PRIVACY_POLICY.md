# Privacy Policy for ezy Youtube Video Control

**Last updated: July 25, 2026**

## 1. Overview
**ezy Youtube Video Control** ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices regarding the Chrome Extension.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
- **NO Personal Data Collected**: We do NOT collect, store, track, sell, or transmit any personal identification information, browsing history, web activity, or user communications.
- **Local Storage Only**: All user preferences (such as selected video aspect ratios, flip settings, mouse wheel control options, cinema dimming opacity, and custom keyboard shortcuts) are stored locally on your device using Chrome's `chrome.storage.sync` API. This data never leaves your browser and is never sent to any external servers.

## 3. Permissions Used
- **`https://www.youtube.com/*` (Host Permission)**: Used exclusively to access YouTube video elements (`video.html5-main-video`) to apply aspect ratio scaling, video flips, mouse wheel controls, and cinema mode dimming overlays.
- **`storage`**: Used to save your settings locally in your browser.

## 4. Remote Code Policy
The extension does NOT execute, fetch, or load any remote code. All code and scripts are 100% packaged locally within the extension bundle.

## 5. Third-Party Sharing
We do NOT sell, trade, or transfer any data to third parties.
